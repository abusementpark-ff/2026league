Grade of the Week

Hellen Keller's Dodgeball Squad
Proj 228.2 → Actual 269.0 (+40.8) · Grade A+ · Result: W

Ryan scored 269.0 on a 228.22 projection and beat David (258.7) in the highest-scoring game of the week. Trevor Lawrence scored 54.2, the highest score in the league, Jayden Daniels scored 45.0 at OP, Tre Tucker scored 31.45 on a 13.01 projection, and the Ravens D/ST scored 25.0.
