Covfefe is for Closers — David Pannell
Projected: 4914.8 pts · Grade: A+

Ja'Marr Chase and Jaxon Smith-Njigba to open, then Saquon Barkley and Jeremiyah Love, because apparently David wanted a running back room good enough to make Barkley the backup plan. Worst Draft

Nabers ate my Zombies — Jonathan Jaramillo
Projected: 4484.1 pts · Grade: D

Credit where it's due, briefly: Jonathan runs a team literally named after Malik Nabers, and he actually drafted Malik Nabers — landed at pick 47, right where he belonged. Everything after reads like he lost a bet: two shaky, streamer-tier quarterbacks in a format that requires two real ones, a Tyrone Tracy Jr. reach 77 spots ahead of his value, a Rams Defense reach 86 spots ahead of its value, and — because two defenses apparently wasn't enough of a bit — a THIRD defense (Buccaneers) burned on a bench spot nobody will ever use. One correct pick, surrounded by a landfill.

🏆 The Uncle Rico Award — Most Boneheaded Pick

Catchy Football Name — Ryan McCarty
Jayden Daniels, Round 1, Pick 8 overall · Consensus rank: 55th

Pick 8 overall. Seven players had come off the board — Nacua, Walker, Bijan, Achane, Chase, Henry, Gibbs — a murderer's row of guys who actually win you fantasy games, and Ryan looked at all of it and threw deep for a quarterback ranked 55th overall. Jayden Daniels, by every projection in this dataset, was a round-5 or round-6 value — the kind of player you get for free while everyone else is still drafting real starters. Taking him at 1.8 means reaching 47 spots ahead of his actual worth, in the single most expensive round of the draft to be wrong in. If you coulda gone State, you coulda been a star — instead you spent the 8th pick of the entire draft throwing a spiral into the side of a moving minivan, and somehow you're still walking around with the highest point total in the league to show for it. That's not skill. That's the football gods taking pity on you.
